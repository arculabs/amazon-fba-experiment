## Electric stapler exact niche

The `electric stapler` detail export contains 20 meaningful search terms totaling approximately **658,113 searches over the past 360 days**.

Weighted across the supplied search terms:

- approximate search conversion rate: **6.1%**
- weighted 90-day search-volume growth: **+5.0%**
- weighted 180-day search-volume growth: **+12.0%**

The main query `electric stapler` alone shows:

- 328,463 searches / 360 days
- +14.54% 180-day growth
- 6.33% search conversion
- Amazon Basics click share: **50.97%**

Competition is highly concentrated:

- Amazon Basics ASIN `B0DTP1S9DY` appears among the top 3 clicked products for **17 of 20** supplied search terms.
- Bostitch ASIN `B00006JNU2` appears for **16 of 20**.
- Bostitch ASIN `B00006JNU3` appears for **9 of 20**.

Decision: **REJECT electric staplers as the first ARCU sourcing niche.**

Reason:

1. Amazon's own private-label product dominates the primary query.
2. Bostitch also has entrenched brand concentration.
3. The category adds electrical/battery/AC complexity that the first-experiment product profile intentionally prefers to avoid.
4. Strong demand alone does not create a sourcing advantage; ARCU would need unusually favorable brand-direct authorization/pricing to compete against Amazon Basics and entrenched Bostitch listings.

Do not spend supplier-research time on electric staplers unless a direct brand opportunity appears independently.

## Most interesting remaining stapler-family signal

The cleanest standout in the broader export is **`staplers bulk`**:

- search volume, past 360 days: **23,076**
- search growth, past 180 days: **+42.42%**
- search volume, past 90 days: **7,611**
- search growth, past 90 days: **+28.0%**
- units sold, past 360 days: **4,000–5,000**
- top clicked products: **18**
- average price: **$28.57**
- observed price range: **$8.85–$52.38**
- return rate: **0.22%**

This is much closer to ARCU's current target profile than ordinary staplers: ASP is above $25, returns are very low, competition is not excessively fragmented, and search direction is positive.

It is **not yet a candidate**. Bulk packaging may increase FBA dimensional/weight costs, and we still need the detailed niche view to understand the actual products, brands, click concentration, conversion rate, and whether the apparent demand is clean rather than category contamination.

Next bounded action: export/open the **`staplers bulk`** niche details before exploring more Office Product branches.

## Data-quality caution

The `paperpro stapler` row shows an average price of **$132.67** with a maximum of **$761.43**, which is inconsistent with ordinary stapler economics and likely reflects mixed/outlier products. Do not treat that row as an attractive high-ASP niche without detailed inspection.
