Amazon's `insulated pliers` niche is semantically mixed.

The top three clicked products are ordinary Klein long-nose pliers rather than insulated 1000V/VDE tools. That means the broad niche metrics overstate the role of generic Klein pliers and understate the economics of the true insulated-tool subset.

- search conversion: approximately **3.14%**

### True insulated subset

Filtering the product titles for explicit insulated / 1000V / VDE products produces **22 ASINs** representing approximately **40.37%** of niche click share.

For that true insulated subset:

- click-share-weighted ASP: approximately **$64.11**

This is substantially stronger than the broad niche headline.
