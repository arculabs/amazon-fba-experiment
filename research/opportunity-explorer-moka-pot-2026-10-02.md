## Interpretation

### Generic moka pot

Advance this one to detail.

Why:

1. **100k–125k annual units** gives enough market depth.
2. **$33.53 ASP** is above the obvious low-ticket failure zone.
3. Only **27 top-clicked products** means the product table should be easy to interpret.
4. Bialetti appears in the top search terms, indicating meaningful established-brand demand rather than a purely private-label category.

Risks:

- 180-day search demand is down **15.46%**;
- the last 90 days are also down **11.47%**, so unlike tortilla press there is not yet a clear stabilization signal;
- the **5.10% return rate** is materially higher than compact hand tools;
- stainless/aluminum size variants may fragment demand.

### Electric moka pot

Do not prioritize despite the $40.38 ASP. It adds electrical complexity and does not offer a large enough economic advantage over the non-electric niche.

### 2-cup / 9-cup / 12-cup variants

These have somewhat higher ASPs but much smaller unit volume. The generic `moka pot` product table should naturally expose the leading Bialetti and other size-specific ASINs, so separate detail exports are unnecessary unless the generic table points to a specific size opportunity.

## Decision

Advance only the exact **`moka pot`** niche to detailed **Products + Search Terms** inspection.

Primary gates:

1. Bialetti and other established-brand click share;
2. seller participation on the leading ASINs;
3. whether higher-ASP Bialetti/stainless variants have enough demand;
4. actual item weight and packaging;
5. whether authorized wholesale access is realistic;
6. whether the niche's 5% return rate is concentrated in certain sizes/materials.

If the detailed market is mostly tightly controlled/private-label or the dominant branded ASINs are low-priced, reject the niche and move to `vinyl record storage`.
