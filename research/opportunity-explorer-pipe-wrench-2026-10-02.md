## Interpretation

### Generic pipe wrench

The generic niche is too broad for first-pass sourcing:

- ASP only **$30.82**
- 69 top-clicked products
- recent 90-day demand down **18.06%**
- likely mixed sizes and weight classes

Do not detail the generic row yet.

### 18-inch pipe wrench

This is the strongest classic pipe-wrench niche:

- **$62.60 ASP**
- **6k–8k units/year**
- only **29 top-clicked products**
- **2.53% return rate**
- top search terms include **RIDGID**, giving a clear established-brand signal

The main concern is demand contraction and physical weight. A detailed product table should quickly show whether RIDGID / Crescent / IRWIN-style listings are broad enough for authorized resale and whether the high-click products remain economical for FBA.

### Chain wrench

This is the most attractive demand-quality signal in the export:

- **$49.74 ASP**
- **4k–5k units/year**
- **+6.55%** 180-day growth
- **+9.26%** 90-day growth
- only **1.09% returns**

The tradeoff is 49 top-clicked products, so the niche may be more fragmented and specialized than the 18-inch pipe-wrench market.

Still, positive growth + low returns + near-$50 ASP make it worth one detail pass.

### Larger 24/36/48-inch pipe wrenches

Do not prioritize. ASP rises with size, but annual volume drops sharply while physical weight almost certainly increases FBA and inbound costs. The 48-inch niche in particular has only 500–750 annual units despite a $149 ASP.

## Decision

Advance exactly two niches to detailed Products + Search Terms inspection:

1. **`pipe wrench 18 inch`**
2. **`chain wrench`**

Do not export detail tabs for the generic pipe-wrench niche or the 24/36/48-inch variants unless these two detail screens reveal a specific brand relationship worth expanding.

Primary gates:

- seller participation on RIDGID / Crescent / IRWIN and other established-brand ASINs;
- exact ASP distribution;
- brand concentration;
- product weight / package size;
- authorized wholesale path and Amazon Marketplace permission.

The defining signal is RIDGID:

- most RIDGID listings show **34–74 sellers/vendors**
- products with more than 10 sellers/vendors represent **85.89% of niche click share**

Strong exact ASINs:

- **RIDGID 31100 / Model 818 Aluminum 18-inch — B0000224JF**
  - 17.77% niche click share
  - ~$75.59 ASP
  - 72 sellers/vendors
  - official RIDGID weight: **3.75 lb**
- **RIDGID 31025 / Model 18 Heavy-Duty — B0000224J9**
  - 6.49% niche click share
  - ~$51.78 ASP
  - 74 sellers/vendors
  - official RIDGID weight: roughly **5.5–5.75 lb**

This is one of the clearest open-listing established-brand structures found in the entire experiment.

However, RIDGID's current Preferred Online Distributor policy is materially more demanding than the small-brand programs screened so far. Public requirements include being an authorized Ridge Tool distributor in good standing, MAP compliance, a distributor-owned website, at least Silver status in RIDGID's PROAction pricing program, monthly point-of-sale reporting, a dedicated RIDGID landing page, and carrying the full RIDGID line online.

That policy does not explicitly answer whether a new distributor may sell on Amazon Marketplace, so marketplace authorization remains the gate. It does signal that RIDGID manages online distribution deliberately and may be a poor fit for a very small first-order account even though the Amazon listings themselves are broad.

**Decision: PASS for a bounded RIDGID channel-access inquiry.** Do not model economics until RIDGID confirms whether Amazon Marketplace resale is permitted and whether a small new reseller can access an authorized supply path without meeting a full-line preferred-online-distributor commitment.
