## Overview

The exact `bread slicer` niche is materially more attractive than cabinet hardware and deserves a detailed inspection.

Key metrics:

- **5,046,641 searches/year**
- **150k–200k units/year**
- **42 top-clicked products**
- **$37.47 average price**
- **1.92% return rate**
- 180-day search growth: **-32.3%**
- 90-day search growth: **-3.46%**

This is a large established market with a workable ASP and low return rate. The main warning is that search demand has contracted sharply over the past 180 days, although the 90-day decline is much smaller, suggesting the drop may be decelerating.

## Data-quality / taxonomy note

The keyword search also returns ordinary bread-food niches such as rye bread, brioche, keto bread, potato bread, etc. Those are irrelevant to this sourcing experiment and should not be interpreted as part of the bread-slicer opportunity.

## Decision

**Advance the exact `bread slicer` niche to detailed Products + Search Terms inspection.**

Why:

1. ASP is high enough at **$37.47** to potentially support the ~$5/unit contribution floor.
2. Return rate is only **1.92%**, far cleaner than cabinet hardware.
3. Demand is very large at **150k–200k units/year**.
4. Only **42 top-clicked products**, which should make brand and ASIN concentration tractable.

Primary risk to test next: whether the niche is dominated by private-label products / one- or two-seller listings, and whether the apparent $37.47 average is supported by the high-click ASINs rather than a few expensive outliers.
