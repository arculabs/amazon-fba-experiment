## Interpretation

### Generic wire stripper

The average price is below ARCU's preferred ~$30+ screening band, but the niche is too strong to discard without seeing the actual ASIN mix:

- 200k–250k annual units;
- only 33 top-clicked products;
- very low 1.32% return rate;
- 90-day search volume has returned to +4.98% growth;
- the top search terms include brand-led intent such as Klein.

A detailed product table may reveal higher-ASP established-brand ASINs with open seller participation even though the niche-wide average is $26.36.

### Automatic wire stripper

This is the cleanest first detail target:

- $39.41 ASP;
- 10k–12.5k annual units;
- only 26 top-clicked products;
- 1.55% return rate;
- recent 90-day growth +4.78%.

The 180-day trend is mildly negative (-6.16%), so this is an established/stable niche rather than a growth bet.

### Insulated pliers

Very attractive headline economics:

- $54.42 ASP;
- low 1.27% return rate;
- positive growth over both 90 and 180 days.

However it is adjacent to the wire-stripper search rather than the core strip-tool niche and has 41 top products. Keep as the next hand-tool test after the stripper detail work.

### Wire stripper machine

The $104.01 ASP and 40k–50k annual units are striking, but this category can include bench-top/manual-crank/electric machinery and heavier equipment. That adds size, mechanical complexity, and potentially electrical product risk inconsistent with the first-experiment preference. Do not advance it yet.

### Brand-specific rows

`Milwaukee wire strippers`, `DEWALT wire stripper`, `Ideal wire strippers`, and `StripMeister` are useful brand-demand signals, not independent sourcing decisions. Exact authorization and seller structure should come from the detailed generic/automatic niche tables before outreach.

## Decision

Advance **two** niches to detailed inspection:

1. `automatic wire stripper` — first priority because ASP is $39.41 with good volume and low returns.
2. `wire stripper` — second priority because the extremely large market and tight 33-product click set may contain accessible branded ASINs even though the niche ASP is only $26.36.

For each, export the **Products** and **Search Terms** tabs.

If neither produces a viable branded/open-wholesale ASIN, test `insulated pliers` next rather than spending time on wire-stripper machines.
