## Decision

**REJECT meatball makers for the first ARCU wholesale/FBA experiment.**

Do not export detail tabs and do not perform supplier outreach.

Reason: even with healthy demand, strong growth, and very low returns, the category's $12–$13 average selling prices leave too little room for the approximately $5/unit contribution floor after referral fees, FBA fulfillment, inbound/prep, and wholesale cost.

This is another useful confirmation that the Unmet Demand Beta surface can identify consumer interest, but does not screen for ARCU's required unit economics.
