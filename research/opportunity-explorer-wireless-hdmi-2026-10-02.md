## Interpretation

### Wireless HDMI

This is strong enough to justify detailed inspection despite being electrical/electronics.

Why it advances:

1. **$62.65 ASP** gives meaningful room for FBA and wholesale margin.
2. **80k–100k annual units** is deep enough for a small wholesale experiment.
3. Only **30 top-clicked products** should make brand/ASIN concentration easy to interpret.
4. **2.97% return rate** is acceptable for an electronics category and much lower than many consumer-electronics niches.
5. Recent demand has rebounded sharply: **+16.65% over 90 days**, even though the 180-day comparison remains **-12.25%**.

Primary risks:

- electronics failure/compatibility/support burden;
- FCC / battery / power-adapter / warranty considerations depending on product design;
- private-label concentration;
- higher return reasons around latency, resolution, range, HDCP/streaming compatibility, and setup expectations;
- possible rapid model turnover.

### 2026-prefixed niche

Ignore the `2026-wireless hdmi transmitter and receiver` row. It has only 250–500 annual units and appears to be a query-specific micro-niche rather than a durable market.

## Decision

Advance only **`wireless hdmi`** to detailed **Products + Search Terms** inspection.

Primary gates:

1. established-brand versus private-label click share;
2. seller participation on the leading ASINs;
3. whether the $62.65 ASP is supported by the dominant products;
4. exact return rate / ratings on leading ASINs;
5. whether products require batteries or unusual compliance handling;
6. whether there is an authorized wholesale path with Amazon Marketplace permission.

If the leading ASINs are mostly one- or two-seller private-label listings, reject the niche quickly despite the strong headline economics.

## Detailed inspection

Seller Central detail exports:

- `NicheDetailsProductsTab_10_2_2026(12).csv`
- `NicheDetailsSearchTermsTab_10_2_2026(12).csv`

Key findings:

- 2,748,264 searches/year across the supplied terms
- weighted search conversion: ~1.13%
- weighted 90-day growth: ~+16.17%
- weighted 180-day growth: ~-12.05%
- top 5 ASINs: 49.50% of clicks
- top 10 ASINs: 72.64%
- click-share-weighted ASP: ~$59.90
- median ASIN ASP: ~$60.55
- 11 of 30 ASINs average more than 3 sellers/vendors, representing ~59.0% of niche click share
- products launched in 2026 represent ~43.65% of click share
- products launched in 2025 or later represent ~60.0% of click share

Largest brands by click share are ANJCTDD, Futureagle, FWTAOU, EVATEK, and Taiquinix. No established national electronics brand dominates the niche. UGREEN and Acer appear, but each represents less than 0.5% of niche click share.

Decision: **REJECT wireless HDMI for the first ARCU wholesale/FBA experiment.**

The ASP and recent demand rebound are attractive, but the category shows rapid product turnover and is led mainly by marketplace electronics brands rather than durable, clearly authorized wholesale relationships. For the first experiment, prioritize stable, replenishable products with longer-lived model identity and clearer brand-direct distribution paths.

Do not perform supplier outreach here.
