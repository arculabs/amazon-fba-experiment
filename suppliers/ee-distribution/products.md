## Entry standard for future research

For each researched product, preserve as much of the following as is available:

- research date;
- exact EE product name and SKU;
- UPC/model number when available;
- stock/orderability and MOQ/case quantity;
- marketplace resale restrictions;
- exact Amazon ASIN;
- observed Amazon price;
- seller count / Amazon presence;
- demand/rank/review evidence;
- estimated Amazon referral and FBA fulfillment fees;
- EE dealer price;
- first-order effective EE cost after discount and $0.50 prep/label fee;
- estimated inbound/other unit costs;
- estimated profit and margin;
- decision status; and
- concise rejection/candidate rationale.

Do not silently fill missing values. Mark them unresolved.
