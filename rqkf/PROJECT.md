# Amazon FBA Experiment — Project Truth

## North star

Determine whether ARCU Labs can source a small, controlled wholesale inventory position from a legitimate distributor and sell it profitably through Amazon FBA with a repeatable, evidence-based sourcing process.

The first experiment is intended to learn, not to maximize scale.

## Current milestone

Select one viable first SKU from EE Distribution and validate the complete landed economics before placing an order.

## Current experiment bounds

- Target initial inventory exposure: roughly $250–$500.
- Prefer the smallest practical order quantity.
- Avoid products whose marketplace resale terms prohibit Amazon.
- Favor products that are small/light enough for manageable FBA fulfillment economics.
- Require an exact product identity match between distributor listing and Amazon ASIN.
- Current working screen is approximately $5+ realistic profit per unit after known supplier and Amazon costs.
- Do not let sunk research effort justify a marginal SKU.

## Research sequence

1. Distributor item is in stock / practically orderable.
2. Marketplace resale is permitted.
3. Exact SKU/UPC/model is matched to the exact Amazon ASIN.
4. Review Amazon price, demand/rank signals, review depth, seller count, Amazon presence, and price stability where available.
5. Estimate referral fee, FBA fulfillment, distributor prep/labeling, inbound shipping, and other known per-unit costs.
6. Calculate the maximum viable distributor buy price.
7. Compare that threshold with the actual dealer price.
8. Record the outcome in the supplier product ledger before moving on.

## Evidence standard

A product is not a candidate merely because its retail price looks attractive.

Research must distinguish:

- verified facts;
- current observations that can change;
- estimates/assumptions; and
- unresolved information.

Exact SKU/ASIN matching and current supplier restrictions are hard gates.

## Repository role

This repository is the durable continuity layer for the experiment. Chat sessions may conduct research, but durable conclusions, supplier terms, and product outcomes belong here.

Large research batches should be checkpointed to the repository in small increments so a session expiration cannot erase the whole batch.
