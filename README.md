# Amazon FBA Experiment

A durable research and decision ledger for ARCU Labs' small-scale Amazon FBA sourcing experiment.

This repository exists so product research, supplier terms, rejections, candidates, and final decisions survive individual chat or research sessions.

## Current stage

The experiment has completed an initial EE Distribution sourcing study and is now testing a **market-first, brand-authorized wholesale** approach.

The Amazon Seller account is now active. Remaining Seller Central onboarding items shown as critical are deposit-method readiness, tax information, and return-address review.

Current objective: complete those account prerequisites, then use Amazon-native market data (starting with Product Opportunity Explorer) to identify one low-risk first SKU with durable demand, legitimate/documentable supply, and enough absolute contribution to justify the operating effort. EE remains an available supplier rather than the project boundary.

## Working constraints

- Initial inventory exposure target: roughly **$250–$500 total**.
- Prefer the smallest practical order quantity.
- Supplier should be able to ship inventory toward Amazon FBA without ARCU Labs physically handling each unit.
- Marketplace resale must be explicitly allowed.
- Exact distributor SKU / UPC must be matched to the exact Amazon ASIN before economics are trusted.
- Current working profitability screen: approximately **$5+ realistic profit per unit** after distributor cost, Amazon fees, supplier prep/labeling, and reasonable inbound costs.
- Research conclusions must be written to this repository before moving on to large additional research batches.
